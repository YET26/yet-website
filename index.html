Who We Are

A community initiative built on showing up for each other

Yemenis Excel Together is a community initiative dedicated to providing free tutoring, ESL classes, and mentorship for Yemeni students and women. Our programs focus on creating small, supportive, and consistent learning environments that help our community learn, grow, and succeed together.

Learn More
Our Mission & Values

Every individual deserves the chance to thrive

We believe every individual has the potential to thrive when given access to quality education and support. Our mission is to expand educational opportunities within the Yemeni community by providing free tutoring, ESL classes, and mentorship that empower students and women to learn, grow, and succeed.
